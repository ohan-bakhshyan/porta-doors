01 / Architecture

Solid Core Construction

Engineered multi-layer acoustic core stabilized with perimeter hardwood stiles and extruded aluminum alignment channels to prevent warping across ceiling-height spans.

02 / Advantages

Key Benefits

  • Sits 100% flush with drywall, plaster, or wood wall paneling without bulky trim casings.
  • Factory pre-milled for concealed 3D adjustable hinges and silent magnetic mortise locks.
  • High acoustic attenuation for bedrooms, offices, and private suites.
03 / Installation

Good to Know

Concealed aluminum subframes install directly onto wood or metal studs prior to final drywall mudding. Compatible with single swing, pocket cavity, bypass, and concealed barn hardware.
